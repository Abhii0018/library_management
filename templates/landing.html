Circulation Architecture

How Digital Borrowing Operates

Borrowing at LibriCore is designed to be rigorous, transparent, and completely paperless.

Stage I

Catalog Inquest

Search our comprehensive database by title, author, or discipline. Verify live stack counts and inspect complete bibliographical records.

Stage II

Digital Requisition

Specify your borrowing duration (1 to 30 calendar days) and lodge your requisition instantly. No physical ledger queuing or paper requisitions.

Stage III

Archival Issuance

Once confirmed by library staff, the loan period begins immediately. Present your member ID at the main circulation desk to collect the physical volume.

Reading Room Hours & Reader Policies

Our doors and reference facilities operate on an institutional academic schedule.

Reading Room Schedule

Monday - Friday: 8:00 AM - 9:00 PM
Saturday: 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM
Sunday & Holidays: Reading Room open 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM.

Loan Quotas & Terms

Registered members may hold up to 3 concurrent volumes for periods of up to 30 calendar days per requisition.

Borrowing Fee & Fine Policy

First 2 days are free. Day 3 onward incurs ₹20.00 per day, with an additional ₹100.00 penalty if borrowing duration exceeds 5 days.

Inquiries & Reference Desk

Inquire regarding volume acquisitions or special access: circulation@libricore.edu or internal telephone extension 4402.
