Simplified Workflow

How Digital Borrowing Works

Borrowing books at LibriCore is designed to be frictionless, transparent, and completely paperless.

Step 01

Explore & Find

Search our comprehensive catalog by title, author, or discipline. Check real-time shelf copy counts and preview volume descriptions.

Step 02

1-Click Request

Choose your borrowing timeframe (1 to 30 days) and submit your request. There is no complicated paperwork or physical queuing.

Step 03

Instant Issuance

When approved by library staff, the loan begins immediately. Collect your physical copy from the reception desk anytime.

Reader Information

Library Hours & Reader Policies

Our doors are open to support research, quiet contemplation, and group discussions.

Operating Hours

Monday – Friday: 8:00 AM – 9:00 PM
Saturday: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM
Sunday & Holidays: Reading room access only (10:00 AM – 4:00 PM).

Loan Allowances

Enrolled members can borrow up to 3 volumes simultaneously for periods up to 30 calendar days per request.

Fair Fine Policy

Overdue volumes incur a nominal fine of $1.00 per day past the due date. Settle balances easily at the circulation desk.

Inquiries & Support

Need a book not in our index? Reach out to our research desk at circulation@libricore.edu or call ext. 4402.
